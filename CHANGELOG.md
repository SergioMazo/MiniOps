## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Conservative installation and preflight framework
- Optional SSH, UFW, Fail2ban, auditd, and AppArmor helpers
- Encrypted Restic backup, integrity, freshness, retention, and restore testing
- Hardened systemd services and timers
- Terminal status dashboard and lightweight health checks
- Security, architecture, recovery, and incident runbooks
- Bash tests, secret scanning, ShellCheck, and Markdownlint workflows
