# Code of Conduct

MiniOps welcomes respectful technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- be considerate and specific;
- critique ideas and code, not people;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's infrastructure data;
- accept good-faith correction;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, and unwanted personal attention.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these
expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Reports should be made privately through the repository's security
reporting channel when safety or privacy is involved.
